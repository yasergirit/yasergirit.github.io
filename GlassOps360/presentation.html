Data quality: verify or block

Source contracts prevent invalid records from silently contaminating business KPIs.

Contract rules

What must the source guarantee?

✓Required columns — expected schema
✓Business keys — non-null and unique
✓Value checks — valid dates and non-negative numbers
✓References — known factories and products
